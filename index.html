01 / About I&D

Technology built around real-world needs.

I&D Intelligence & Data Pvt. Ltd. is an Ahmedabad-based technology company focused on developing AI-driven products and data-oriented solutions.

Our work spans healthcare, medical imaging, clinical decision support, AYUSH applications and legal technology. We take a practical, responsible approach to development—making clear what a system is designed to do, where it is in its development journey, and where professional judgment remains essential.

Our mission

To develop practical, responsible AI tools that support professionals, improve workflows and enable data-informed decisions.

03 / Our approach

Responsible by design.

Useful AI begins with clear scope, careful development and honest communication about limitations.

01

Human-led decisions

In high-impact settings, AI should support—not replace—the judgment of qualified professionals.

02

Clear development stages

We aim to communicate whether a technology is in research, prototype, testing or deployment, without overstating its maturity.

03

Privacy-aware thinking

Responsible technology includes appropriate handling of information, access controls and attention to data protection.
