We are an Ahmedabad-based technology company exploring useful, responsible applications of AI and data across healthcare and other knowledge-intensive fields.

Our company

I&D Intelligence & Data Pvt. Ltd. brings an applied, multidisciplinary perspective to product development.

Our portfolio includes healthcare AI, medical imaging, clinical decision support, AYUSH-focused innovation and legal technology. We focus on practical use cases, clear product boundaries and responsible development.

Our mission

To build practical AI tools that help professionals make better-informed decisions and improve workflows while keeping people at the centre of high-impact decisions.

Our principles

  • Clear and honest communication about capability and maturity.
  • Human oversight where decisions may affect people.
  • Privacy-aware data practices and appropriate safeguards.
  • Iterative development, testing and feedback.
